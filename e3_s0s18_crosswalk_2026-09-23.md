## Honest scope notes

- S2–S17's "n" values are **not new experiments**: they are sub-samples of
  the single N=2000 E1 campaign already executed and reported in
  `e1_formal_benchmark_2026-09-23.md`, filtered post hoc by the case
  attribute matching each scenario's description. Because E1's cases were
  generated stratified/randomly (not scenario-targeted), these counts are
  incidental sample sizes, not designed statistical power; they should be
  read as "0 unsafe acceptances observed in every relevant slice of the
  generated corpus," not as a scenario-by-scenario adversarial red-team
  exercise.
- S4/S6/S7/S16 share their evidence row with an adjacent scenario (S2, S5,
  S5, S15 respectively) because the current formal-benchmark generator does
  not model a field-level distinction between those pairs (e.g. "obligation
  weakening" vs. "obligation stripping" both collapse to "obligation profile
  non-empty, not discharged" in `e1_generator.py`). A future revision of the
  generator could add that distinction; it was not built for this pass.
- S18 has real n=1 (a single, carefully constructed real counterexample on
  live infrastructure), not a corpus. This matches the existing multi-hop
  evidence's own known limit (n=2 hops only), already tracked as the E9 gap.
- S0/S1 are positive controls (expected outcome is direct reuse, not
  rejection); "PASS" for these means the classifier correctly granted
  Exact/Safe, not that it rejected anything.
