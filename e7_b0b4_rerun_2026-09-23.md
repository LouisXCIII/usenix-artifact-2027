Real result for `Manuscriptsp_m2_r3.tex`'s `subsec:results-baselines`
(Table `tab:baseline-security-results`). No new experiments were run: the
same real 24-case corpus already used for the Stage 4/X1/trustless work
(`evidence/trustless_corpus_c1_results_2026-09-21.json` -- 11 real

real Besu light-client header) was reprocessed under r3's **new** B0--B4
definitions, which differ from the old (Stage 4) ones:

| | Old (Stage 4, `Manuscriptsp2_Full_v1.tex`) | New (r3) |
|---|---|---|
| B0 | Always accept (provenance-only, degenerate) | Foreign proof/finality only |
| B1 | Full destination re-eval | Full destination re-eval (same) |
| B2 | Syntactic role mapping only (ignores amount) | Semantic mapping only (role **and** amount, ignoring proof-binding/assurance) |

## Ground truth

Derived from the corpus's own real, on-chain-verified fields (`semBound`,
`evOK` -- read back from the chaincode *after* the real
`SubmitDecisionTrustless` invocation, i.e. genuinely proof-bound and
assurance-checked, not the original naive scanner-only flags used at
Stage 4) plus role/amount vs. the real `RoleThreshold` table:
$\mathrm{gt} = \mathrm{REJECT}$ if `!semBound` or `!evOK`; else
role-unknown $\Rightarrow$ REEVALUATE; else amount$\le$threshold
$\Rightarrow$ SAFE else REJECT. Ground-truth distribution: **5 SAFE, 14
REJECT, 5 REEVALUATE** (n=24).

## Interpretation

- **$B_0$ vs $B_4$**: FAR drops from 100% to 0%. Every one of the 24 cases
  submits *some* cryptographically valid ZK proof (per the corpus's own `zk`
  field detail -- a substituted proof "verifies but its public
  solidityCodeHash differs from this decision's codeHash"), so a baseline
  that checks only proof validity + finality and not proof-to-decision
  binding accepts everything, including every proof-substitution attack in
  the corpus. This is the strongest, most concrete demonstration yet of why
  treating a valid foreign proof as authorization is dangerous.
- **$B_2$ vs $B_4$**: FAR drops from 36.8% to 0%. Checking only role/amount
  compatibility (ignoring whether the proof genuinely binds to the decision,
  i.e. ignoring `semBound`/`evOK`) lets through 7 of 19 unsafe cases --
  exactly the cases where role/amount happen to fall within the destination
  policy's numeric bounds despite a proof-substitution or assurance failure.
- **$B_3$ vs $B_4$**: FAR is small but non-zero (1/19 = 5.3%), isolating
  exactly the assurance-continuity contribution: one real case
  (`SafeBank.sol`, director/\$700,000, `semBound=true` but `evOK=false`)
  is correctly rejected under full ADP but incorrectly accepted once the
  assurance check is dropped. This directly matches the paper's own
  Theorem~3/Corollary~1 framing.
- **$B_1$ vs $B_4$**: identical (FAR=FRR=0 for both) -- the real,
  independently-verified `SubmitDecisionTrustless` result exactly matches
  the strongest local semantic reference on this corpus, a genuine (not
  circular) positive result since $B_1$ is a formula-based local
  recomputation and $B_4$ is a real on-chain proof-and-light-client-verified
  invocation.

## Honest scope notes

- $n=24$ (23 usable after excluding one malformed entry, consistent with
  prior sessions' handling of this same file).
- Ground truth here is derived from the corpus's own real fields, not from
  an independent oracle (unlike E1's Z3-based benchmark). It is defensible
